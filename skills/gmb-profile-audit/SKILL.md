# Google Business Profile Local SEO Audit

Audit any Google Business Profile listing for maximum Local Map Pack visibility.

## What this skill does:
1. Calculates a 0–100 profile completeness score based on Google's algorithmic ranking factors.
2. Identifies missing **Primary & Secondary Categories** using Google's official taxonomy.
3. Discovers high-intent local search keywords (e.g. *"service near me"*, localized modifier terms).
4. Crafts an SEO-optimized 750-character business description integrating local search terms.
5. Produces an immediate 3-step action checklist to leapfrog competitor map rankings.

## How to run:

```bash
python3 /Users/lucifer/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/ApexGMB/skills/scripts/gmb_ai_runner.py audit \
  --name "Apex Media Studio" \
  --category "Photography Studio" \
  --city "New Delhi" \
  --rating 4.8 \
  --reviews 125
```

## Review Checklist:
- **NAP Consistency**: Does Name, Address, and Phone match the official website and local citations exactly?
- **Category Coverage**: Has the business added 3-5 relevant secondary categories?
- **Hours & Special Hours**: Are holiday schedules and regular business hours filled out?
- **Photo Velocity**: Does the profile have at least 20+ recent high-resolution images?

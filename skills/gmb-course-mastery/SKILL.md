# Google Business Profile Advanced Course Mastery

Comprehensive operational rules, ranking algorithm factors, and suspension prevention guidelines from the full GBP masterclass.

## Core Ranking Factors (The Map Pack Trinity)

1. **Relevance**:
   - Primary Category (Single biggest algorithmic ranking factor).
   - Secondary Categories (Cluster up to 5-9 closely related sub-categories).
   - Services Catalog: Write 300-character keyword descriptions for every single service.
   - Pre-seeded Google Q&A (Voice search & conversational query ranking).
2. **Distance / Proximity**:
   - Physical address vs. Searcher GPS.
   - For SABs (Service Area Businesses): Set radius strictly within realistic travel zones (maximum 2 hours driving radius / 20-30 miles). Never spam entire countries or unrelated states.
3. **Prominence**:
   - Review Velocity: Steady incoming stream of authentic reviews containing photos and keywords.
   - Owner Response Rate: 100% response rate within 24–48 hours.
   - Activity Signals: Weekly Google Posts (What's New, Offers, Events).

## Suspension Prevention Rules

- **Name Spamming Risk**: NEVER add keywords, city names, or phone numbers to the business title unless they appear on your legal registration and physical storefront signage. (e.g. *"Dr. John Smith - Best Dentist In Chicago"* will trigger algorithmic suspension).
- **Address Changes**: Changing address or phone number often triggers instant re-verification (video verification or postcard).
- **Service Area Businesses (SAB)**: If you don't have permanent storefront signage with staff during stated hours, you MUST hide your physical address and operate as a Service Area Business.

## Google Q&A Pre-seeding Protocol

Google allows anyone (including the business owner) to ask and answer questions on their own listing:
1. Post the question from a personal Google account or the owner account.
2. Answer from the verified Business Owner profile.
3. Pre-seed questions covering:
   - Pricing / Quotation turnaround
   - Working hours & weekend availability
   - Parking / Accessibility
   - Service locations / Home visits
   - Payment modes accepted (Cash, Cards, UPI, Online)

# Local SEO Agency Client Pitch & Retainer Blueprint

Turn free audits into paying local business clients on recurring retainers.

## What this skill does:
1. Translates technical GBP audit findings (missing categories, low review velocity, unclaimed listings, stale posts) into lost revenue in plain business language.
2. Structures a 30-day "quick win" onboarding sprint:
   - Day 1-7: Complete profile overhaul & category optimization.
   - Day 8-15: Review request generation campaign via QR code & SMS.
   - Day 16-30: Weekly post scheduling & competitor rank tracking.
3. Packages the solution into a clear monthly recurring retainer ($500 – $1,500/month).
4. Outlines clear client deliverables (calls generated, direction requests, search impressions).

# Autonomous AI Agent OS: SOPs & Observe-Think-Act Architecture

## Core Philosophy: The Shift from Prompts to Operating Systems
Stop typing single prompts into chat boxes and starting from zero every session. Build an **Agent Operating System** that maintains persistent context, operates on codified Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and runs the autonomous **Observe-Think-Act** loop.

## 1. The Observe-Think-Act Loop for Google Maps Listings
- **OBSERVE**:
  - Scan the active Google Business Profile on Google Maps.
  - Ingest: Listing Title, Primary Category, Hidden Secondary Categories, Review Score, Total Review Count, Website Presence, Phone Status, Opening Hours, Post Recency, and Photo Count.
- **THINK**:
  - Compare the observed metrics against the target industry standard (e.g., Photography, Dental, Real Estate).
  - Identify the single highest-leverage bottleneck:
    - *Is the category misplaced?* (Instant ranking ceiling).
    - *Is review velocity stagnant?* (Competitors overtaking).
    - *Are reviews missing keywords and city names in owner replies?* (Lost algorithmic weight).
    - *Are posts older than 7 days?* (Decayed freshness score).
- **ACT**:
  - Execute the corresponding standard operating procedure (SOP).
  - Produce immediate, production-ready deliverables: category reassignment recommendation, geo-optimized review reply drafts, and ready-to-post weekly updates.

## 2. Skills as SOPs (Executable Standard Operating Procedures)
Instead of re-explaining rules to the AI every day:
- **Brand Voice SOP**: Define exact tone, forbidden phrases (e.g. "delve", "testament", "unlock"), and required local keywords.
- **Review Reply SOP**:
  - 5-Star: Thank customer, mention the specific service (e.g. "wedding photography", "pre-wedding shoot"), name the city/locality, and invite back.
  - 1-Star / Negative: Apologize with empathy, avoid taking blame publicly, provide a direct manager contact phone/email, and do not repeat negative accusation keywords.
- **Google Update SOP**:
  - Catchy local headline with emoji.
  - Value snippet or seasonal promotion.
  - Direct Call To Action (Book, Call Now, Learn More).

## 3. Self-Improving Business Memory
- Store continuous client feedback and domain guidelines in persistent storage (`memory.json` / `chrome.storage.sync`).
- When a business owner modifies an AI suggestion or specifies a constraint ("Never mention passport photos", "Always highlight cinematic wedding packages"), the agent commits this preference into its persistent memory.
- Every subsequent generation automatically adheres to these learned rules.

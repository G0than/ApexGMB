# Weekly Google Posts Generation Engine

Maintain an active, high-ranking Google Business Profile without spending hours writing updates.

## What this skill does:
1. Plans a 4-week thematic posting calendar:
   - **Week 1: Core Service Highlight** (Showcase a flagship offering with benefits).
   - **Week 2: Social Proof / Case Study** (Celebrate a customer story or behind-the-scenes shoot).
   - **Week 3: Limited-Time Promotion / Offer** (Coupon, seasonal discount, or special package).
   - **Week 4: Educational / Pro-Tip** (Helpful advice establishing local authority).
2. Provides compelling copy (100–150 words) structured for high mobile engagement.
3. Suggests explicit Call-To-Action (CTA) buttons: `Call Now`, `Book`, `Learn More`, or `Order Online`.
4. Suggests the optimal photo/image to accompany the post.

## How to run:

```bash
python3 /Users/lucifer/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/ApexGMB/skills/scripts/gmb_ai_runner.py posts \
  --business "Apex Media Studio" \
  --category "Photography Studio" \
  --city "New Delhi" \
  --offer "15% off Wedding and Pre-Wedding packages booked this month"
```

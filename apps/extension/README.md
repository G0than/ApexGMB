# MapRank Everywhere - Local SEO & Audit Engine

An all-in-one browser extension for Chrome, Brave, and Edge that inspects Google Business Profile (GBP) categories directly on Google Maps, audits local competitors, performs deep local SEO analysis, and automates high-converting marketing workflows via AI.

## Features

- **Google Maps Category & CID Inspector**:
  - Automatically uncovers and badges **Primary Categories** on map search results and business details.
  - Reveals hidden secondary categories and single-click copy for **CID** (Google Customer ID).
- **⚡ Instant 100-Point GBP Audit**:
  - Evaluates listing completeness, category signal strength, review velocity, and contact point integrity.
- **💼 1-Person Agency & Client Acquisition Suite**:
  - Generates audit-gap cold emails with high-response subject lines and zero-friction video audit CTAs.
  - Crafts winning Upwork proposals and calculates custom 3-Tier Retainer packages ($500 / $1,500 / $3,000/mo).
  - Supplies discovery call closing scripts and objection handlers.
- **⚡ Autonomous Agent OS (Observe-Think-Act)**:
  - Scans live listing signals, diagnoses algorithmic vulnerabilities, and outputs executable SOPs.
  - Persistent business memory saves brand tone, keywords, and client constraints.
- **📊 4S Proof of Work & Showcase Engine**:
  - Turns profile audits into authoritative client teardown case studies demonstrating before/after financial returns.
- **🛡️ Q&A Pre-Seed & Policy Protection**:
  - Generates geo-targeted Q&A pairs to capture voice search and drafts policy dispute tickets for fake review removal.
- **📱 Social Media & Ads Repurposing Hub**:
  - Transforms customer reviews into 30-second viral Reels/TikTok scripts, carousels, and Meta ad hooks.
- **📍 Teleport / Local Geo Simulator**:
  - Simulates search queries from any GPS coordinate or neighborhood center to test local map pack rankings.

## Installation

1. Open your browser and go to `chrome://extensions/` (or `brave://extensions/` / `edge://extensions/`).
2. Toggle on **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
3. Click **Load unpacked**.
4. Select the extension folder:
   ```
   apps/extension
   ```
5. Click the extension icon in your browser toolbar, switch to the **⚙️ Settings** tab, and enter your **OpenRouter API Key** (e.g. `sk-or-v1-...`). Click **Save Settings**.
6. Navigate to [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps) and search for any local business. The MapRank Everywhere badges, AI audit tools, and floating control panel will appear automatically.
